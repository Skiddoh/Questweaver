# Two-player Forever-client checklist

1. Install the addon on two characters, log both in, and confirm `/qw` opens, moves, closes, and shows an empty 32-slot RP Satchel. Confirm the bag icon opens Inventory, the quest-note icon opens accepted active quests, and the NPC icon or `/qw interact` opens a separate panel. Verify the panel has expandable New, Active, Completed, Repeat, and Custom Dialogue sections; reward turn-ins appear under Active, completed quests can be accepted again under Repeat, and selecting a new quest shows its dialogue before the explicit accept action. Confirm all three icons move with Shift + left-drag.
1b. Accept a Questweaver quest. Confirm its title and current objective appear in the on-screen **Questweaver** tracker section below the Blizzard objective tracker; kill objectives show a live `current/required` counter. Collapse and expand the section, Shift + left-drag its heading to reposition it, then `/reload` and confirm the position persists. Finish an objective and confirm the section refreshes. Verify Blizzard's regular Quest Log is unchanged.
1c. Confirm the RP Satchel shortcut uses the updated bag icon. The coin shortcut shows the exact `Questweaver RP Token` balance (including `0`); award/remove/trade tokens and confirm the displayed count refreshes. Shift + left-drag the coin counter, `/reload`, and verify its position persists.
1d. Target an NPC, open `/qw interact`, and verify the window closes after changing target or moving outside normal NPC interaction distance. Move back into range and open it again; confirm no coordinates are required. Repeat during combat to ensure there is no blocked-action message.
1e. In Inventory, click an item and confirm its slot gets a clear gold outline; click another and confirm the outline moves. Right-click to insert a chat link without changing selection. Remove the selected item's last quantity and confirm the outline clears.
1f. Confirm the NPC interaction shortcut is the same 42px footprint as the other quick buttons, uses the note icon (not the old white gossip glyph), and has no text printed over the art; hover it to confirm its tooltip still identifies NPC interactions.
1g. Create one item per category. Confirm creation adds definitions to **Maker**, not quantity to the RP Satchel. Grant `misc`, `weapon`, `armor`, and `food` items from Maker to the Satchel, respecting max stack. Confirm `quest item` and `reward` cannot be added from Maker; collect/loot and quest completion can award them. Trade an eligible satchel item and confirm it remains in the maker definition list.
1h. Select and delete an unused Maker definition using the two-click confirmation. Confirm deletion is blocked for the default token, an item in the Satchel, and items referenced by quests or Sets. Create a Set and quest; confirm their delete actions require confirmation, Set deletion preserves quests/items, and quest deletion is blocked while referenced by a Set or an open run. Remove the reference/run and confirm deletion succeeds.
1a. Create an RP item with a stack of 3. Select it in Inventory, enter quantity `2`, click **Remove selected** once and confirm nothing changed, then click again and confirm the stack is 1. Repeat with quantity `1`; the inventory slot should become empty but the item definition should still be available in Create. Confirm an amount greater than the owned stack and a fractional quantity do not remove anything.
2. On A click **Create sealed-letter demo**. In Inventory copy the `item:` ID of Sealed Letter; the generated quest ID remains in Create.
3. A runs `/qw trade`, clicks the Sealed Letter in the trade window's RP inventory row, enters B's character name and quantity `1`, then chooses **Offer / update**. Confirm B's trade window opens with the letter under **Their offer**.
4. B and A press **Confirm** in their trade windows. Confirm the letter disappears from A only after B receives it, and both trade panels show `complete`.
5. A opens **Quests**, selects the demo's **YOUR QUEST** entry, enters B's character name in **Share with**, and clicks **Share quest**. On B, confirm the entry is labelled **ACTIVE**, then use **Progress objective** for dialogue and possession, then delivery. Confirm the letter is removed from B and appears once in A's RP inventory.
5a. In the Quest Journal, verify the expandable **Your Quests**, **Completed**, and **Open Quests** sections. Selecting a quest authored by you should show **Edit quest** and **Share quest**; editing opens that quest in the composer. Selecting an offer or another player's quest should show its details and relevant accept/progress actions. Expand another section and confirm it collapses the previous section; use `/qw active` to open directly to Open Quests.
5b. Create an NPC-given quest with **Repeatable: No**, complete it, and verify it appears under **Completed** but not **Repeat Quests**; attempting a repeat action must leave it completed. Edit it, switch **Repeatable: Yes**, save, and share/offer it to the second player. After completion there, verify **Repeat Quests** offers it, accepting again resets it to objective 1, and the received definition preserves the repeatable setting.
5c. Create a quest with **Suggested level** set to `12` and **Suggested player count** set to `3`; save, reopen it and confirm both remain set. Share it with the other player and verify both suggestions appear in their quest details and NPC offer details. Confirm blank means **Any**, invalid/fractional values are rejected, and neither suggestion blocks acceptance or progress.
5d. Open the quest editor and confirm it opens on **General**. Verify quest title/reward/locations/suggestions/NPC/repeatability/sharing fields are in General; switch to **Objectives** and verify the ordered objective builder and objective sequence are there. Switch back and forth while editing and confirm entered values persist; save a quest from either section and confirm the quest is created/updated.
5e. Open **NPC conversations** or `/qw conversation`, target an NPC and choose **Use targeted NPC**, write dialogue, and use **Choose quests** to add two authored quest definitions. Save, reopen from the Saved Conversations list, and verify fields persist. Locally, `/qw interact` should show the conversation under **Custom Dialogue** and the linked quests under **New Quests**. Use **Save + share** to send offers to the other player; verify their targeted NPC window shows the dialogue and quests, and quests only activate after explicit acceptance. Delete the conversation and verify its quest definitions remain intact.
6. B completes the manual step to request approval. On A, confirm **RECENT REQUESTS** shows an explicit **Approve with: quest ID | 4 | B-name** line and that the approval field is prefilled. Click **Approve**. Confirm B gets exactly one RP Token and the run is `complete`.
7. Repeat a trade but disconnect B after both confirmations / before acknowledgement. On reconnect reopen `/qw`, confirm A's trade is still `committed`, use **Resend**, and confirm B does not receive a second item.
8. Create and share a quest with `kill~~5~~npc:1984~Defeat five boars`. On the recipient, kill NPC `1984` five times. Confirm the active-quest view changes from `Kills: 0 / 5` through `Kills: 5 / 5`, every counted kill prints its progress in the chat box, and the quest advances without any approval request. Repeat outside an optional configured location to confirm the count does not advance.
9. On A, click **Create Aldrassil boar demo**. Confirm its reward ID is `item:questweaver:rp-token`, share it with B, and kill five NPC `1984` Young Thistle Boars on B, including outside Shadowglen. Confirm each player-credited kill prints progress, the quest becomes `ready_to_turn_in`, then target Conservator Ilthalaine (`2079`) and open the interaction panel. Choose the ready turn-in and confirm B receives exactly one Questweaver RP Token. Confirm the token definition exists on a fresh Questweaver character but has not been granted to its RP Satchel.
10. In **Quests**, click **Remove accepted quests** twice. Confirm accepted active/completed quests disappear but **YOUR QUEST** definitions remain. Create a delivery objective, advance it until delivery acknowledgement is pending, and confirm the removal action is blocked.
11. In the quest editor's **Objectives** tab, click **Add objective** and confirm the type chooser appears. Add two objectives with step number `1` and another with step number `2`. Verify both number-1 objectives are listed as active together and step 2 stays locked until both finish; complete them in either order, then confirm step 2 unlocks. Scroll the list, select a row to edit its number, update it, and remove a row with its **X**. Save/reopen and share the quest; verify step numbers and dependencies persist on the second player. Also confirm a legacy quest with no explicit step numbers retains its original sequential order.
12. Cancel an unconfirmed offer from either player and verify no item quantity changes. Change/reoffer an item and verify both players must confirm again.
13. Send malformed/oversized addon messages from a test addon if available; confirm no Lua errors and no inventory/quest mutation.
14. On A create an RP item named `Librarian's Seal`. Create a real location such as `library~Stormwind City~52~64~3~At the library`, then create/share a quest containing `loot~seal-item-ID~1~library~Dusty Librarian~Search the librarian`. On B, first try advancing in another zone and outside the coordinate radius: it must fail. Move within the radius, target a mob named Dusty Librarian, and advance. Verify the zone/coordinate hint appears, the Seal is added once to B's RP inventory, and a different target cannot complete the step.
15. Create a `Library Book` RP item and a quest step `collect~book-item-ID~1~library~~Take the book from the shelf`. On B, verify collecting outside the library location fails. At the configured position, press **Collect item here** and confirm the book is added exactly once without needing a target.
16. On A create a short quest called `Part 1`, note its quest ID, then create `Part 2` with `Part 1` in **Predecessor quest ID**. Confirm `Part 1` now records `Part 2` as its follow-up. Share `Part 1` with B: only Part 1 should be active. Complete it on B and confirm Part 2 becomes active exactly once, including after reopening `/qw`. Repeat by creating Part 2 first and setting it in Part 1's **Follow-up quest ID** field.
17. On A target a real NPC, create a quest with **Use NPC** and custom **Quest giver dialogue**. Choose **Send NPC offer** to B. Confirm B sees `[NPC OFFER]` in the Quest Journal but has no active quest. Target that NPC and open the interaction panel; verify the offer and custom dialogue appear under the right categories. Acceptance must fail with another target, while the explicit **Accept quest** action adds one active quest when B targets the configured NPC, without checking map coordinates.
18. On a fresh character, target Conservator Ilthalaine (NPC `2079`) in Shadowglen and run `/qw interact`. Confirm the Aldrassil Boar Hunt appears under **New quests** without requiring map coordinates. Select it, read its dialogue, and explicitly accept; confirm it requires five NPC `1984` boar kills. Interact again and confirm it is categorized as in progress rather than offered again.
19. Create two authored quests and one RP item. Open **Create Questweaver Set**, use **Choose quest chapters** to add both quests in order and **Choose supporting items** to add the item, then save. Confirm `/qw sets` shows the Set with both chapter titles and the item. Reopen it, change the chapter order, save, and confirm the same stable Set ID is updated. In the quest editor, right-click a reward, objective-item, follow-up, or predecessor ID field and confirm the browser fills the selected reference.
20. Create a quest with a quest giver, click **Use giver** beside **Reward turn-in NPC ID**, and complete its objectives. Confirm no reward is granted until the player targets that NPC, opens the interaction panel, and chooses the ready turn-in; then confirm the reward is granted exactly once. Repeat with a different NPC ID entered as the turn-in NPC.
21. Target Conservator Ilthalaine and run `/qw npcid`. Confirm the chat box prints `Selected NPC: ... (ID: 2079).` Target a Young Thistle Boar and confirm it prints NPC ID `1984`.
22. Confirm a position objective can be checked without a Blizzard "action blocked" warning, including after entering/leaving combat. If a boar kill fails to count, run `/qw killdebug on` and confirm the event-registration statuses; kill a boar and verify progress from `PARTY_KILL` or combat-log events. Unsupported combat-chat events should not be attempted. Turn diagnostics off with `/qw killdebug off`.
23. Create an RP item with a short description and share its definition with another addon client using the supported trade/quest flow. In Inventory, open chat and right-click its icon; confirm the edit box receives only plain `[Item Name]` text. Send it to one Questweaver client and one client without the addon: the latter should see only the item name; the addon client that knows the item definition should see the icon and name. Click it and verify the preview shows the icon, description, and stable ID without adding the item to inventory. Confirm sending the marker does not produce an invalid escape error.
